01 — Hello there

Not a booth in the corner. A photographer in the middle of it all.

Photo booths wait for guests to come to them. We don't. MemoryCatcher PIX walks your event, snapping the candid laughs, the group hugs and the dance-floor chaos — then prints them right there, on the spot.

Step in front of the green screen and you can be anywhere. Want something to keep on your keys? We'll turn your favorite shot into a personalized photo key fob.

It's photography that becomes part of the party — and a keepsake every guest takes home.
